# Draw Steel: Death Tracker

*The Reaper's digital assistant.*

Draw Steel: Death Tracker is a Foundry VTT module that handles defeat in Draw Steel combat: the fallen stay where they fell, every death is announced in chat with an undo, minion squads lose the right minions, and the Director can raise the dead or finish a fight outright.

## Quick Start

1. Install the module and enable it in your world. Foundry installs Draw Steel: CTLib alongside it.
2. Open **Settings > Module Settings > Draw Steel: Death Tracker** to check the options.
3. Drag the macros you want from the Death Tracker compendium onto your hotbar.

## Playing Well With Others

**[Draw Steel: Target Damage](Target-Damage-Integration)** is recommended: undoing damage that killed a creature brings it back, and dead targets drop out of the card.

**Draw Steel: Combat Tools** is recommended too. With it, squad captains are handed on when they fall, and deaths caused by forced movement undo cleanly.
